ABOUT RETRO ADDA

Retro Adda is a small arcade of classic browser games, made to feel like the PC games we grew up with. Seven games, one click each, nothing to install and no account to make.

Works on phones and computers. Swipe, tap, or use the keyboard.

The site is free. Ad space on the pages keeps it that way. Your high scores and sound setting are saved only in your own browser.
